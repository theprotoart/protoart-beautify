ProtoArt Beautify — INTEL MAC VERSION
=====================================

This copy is for Intel Macs (for example a 2016–2020 MacBook Pro with a Core i5/i7).
Install and first launch work exactly as described in "Read Me First.txt".

WHAT TO EXPECT
  Intel Macs don't have Apple's AI graphics chip, so the AI runs on the processor. It works,
  but it's much slower than on an M-series Mac:
    • INSTANT (sharpen only)  a few minutes (about 2 min for a 1024 px result in testing)
    • TURBO                   roughly 15–30+ minutes per image  ← the app starts on this preset
    • FAST / BALANCED / …     can take an hour or more
  (Estimates: tested by running this Intel version on an M1 Mac; a real Core i5 may differ.)
  Keep Max Size at 768 px, close other apps, and plug in the charger (it works the processor hard
  and the fans will spin up). 16 GB of memory is recommended; 8 GB works but is slower.

  The Light / Color sliders and the Bleach Bypass, Dark Contrasts, Detail Extractor and Film Grain
  filters are instant on any Mac, and you can use them without running the AI at all.
