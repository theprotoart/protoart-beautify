ProtoArt Beautify 1.3 — AI image enhancer for Apple Silicon Macs
=================================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4…) and macOS 13 Ventura or newer
  • About 10 GB of free disk space
  • Internet for the first launch only (it downloads its AI models, about 6 GB, from huggingface.co)

INSTALL
  1. Drag "ProtoArt Beautify" onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Open it from Applications.
  3. macOS will say it can't verify the developer (the app is not from the App Store).
     Click "Done", then open  System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway" next to ProtoArt Beautify, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
     You only need to do this once.
     (Please don't turn off Gatekeeper or use Terminal tricks to skip this step.)

FIRST LAUNCH
  The app downloads and prepares its models (about 6 GB, 10–30 minutes depending on your
  connection). The READY light turns green when it's done. After that it works offline.
  If you use a firewall such as Little Snitch or LuLu, allow ProtoArt Beautify to connect
  to huggingface.co.

USING IT
  Press HOW TO inside the app for a full guide. Quick version:
  OPEN an image → pick a preset (TURBO is fast) → ENHANCE → compare with SINGLE / SPLIT / SIDE
  → SAVE. The ADJUST panel has Light / Color sliders and Bleach Bypass, Dark Contrasts and
  Detail Extractor and Film Grain filters (restack them with the arrows).

PRIVACY
  Everything runs on your Mac. Your images are never uploaded anywhere, and the app sends no
  analytics. The only network use is downloading the AI models on first launch.

UNINSTALL
  Quit the app, drag it to the Trash, and delete the folder
  ~/Library/Application Support/ProtoArt Beautify (the models, about 3 GB).

NOTES
  Models: Juggernaut Reborn (SD 1.5) and a Tile ControlNet (CreativeML OpenRAIL-M),
  4x-UltraSharp (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial), LCM-LoRA (OpenRAIL++). Personal,
  non-commercial use only.
